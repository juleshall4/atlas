## Quick open

Turn on Quick open in **Shortcuts**. Then hold Fn and press a letter to open an app. You can also press Fn, then press the letter within 1 second.

<Setting path={['Shortcuts']} name="Quick open" on />

To give an app a letter:

1. Open **Shortcuts** and select **Apps**.
2. Touch the app.
3. Press the letter.

Each letter opens one app. To give a letter to a different app, first remove it from the app that has it.

To open apps without an animation, turn off **Animation** in **Shortcuts**.

## Actions

Hold Alt and press a key:

- Z to undo
- Y to redo
- A to select all
- C to copy
- X to cut
- V to paste
- Backspace to delete a word

Only Backspace is on at the start. To turn an action on or off, open **Shortcuts** and select **Keys**. When an action is off, Alt and its key type a symbol.
