## Word suggestions and corrections

As you type, the strip above the keyboard shows the words you are most likely typing, and a correction if a word looks misspelled. After a word, it suggests the next one. Touch a word to type it.

To hide the strip, turn off **Suggestion bar** in **Keyboard**.

Atlas corrects spelling in British or American English. To choose, open **Keyboard** and select **Spelling**.

## Capitals and punctuation

Atlas starts each sentence with a capital letter. It also fixes small things as you type, such as "i" to "I", "dont" to "don't", and a space before a full stop or comma.

To type a full stop at the end of a sentence, press the space bar twice.

To turn these off, use **Auto-capitalisation** and **Auto-punctuation** in **Keyboard**.

## Shift and Alt

Press Shift or Alt once and it applies to the next key only. Press it twice quickly to lock it on, then press it again to unlock it.

When Alt is on, the strip shows symbols that are not printed on the keys. Touch one to type it, or swipe left or right on the keys to see more.

You can also hold Alt and press a key for an [action](/keyboard/shortcuts/#edit-text-with-actions), such as undo or copy.

## Emoji

Press Sym to show emoji in the strip, with your recent emoji first. Swipe left or right on the keys to see more, or up or down to move between groups, such as faces and hands. Touch an emoji to type it.

Sym works like Shift: press it once, or press it twice quickly to lock it. Press Backspace to close the emoji.

### Find an emoji

Press Sym, then type a word, such as "pizza". The strip shows emoji that match. Press Enter to type the first one, or touch the one you want. If Sym is locked, the strip stays open for your next search.

If nothing matches, the space bar and Enter type your word as normal text.

### Skin tone

To choose the skin tone for emoji with hands and people, open **Keyboard** and select **Skin tone**.

## Move the cursor

Rest your finger on the space bar until you feel a click, then slide left or right to move the cursor. Lift your finger when the cursor is where you want it.

This works even when swipe typing is off. To turn it off, turn off **Cursor control** in **Keyboard**.

## Delete a whole word

Hold Alt and press Backspace. Ctrl and Backspace does the same.

## Light up the keys

Slide your finger on the keys when you are not typing in a text field, and the keyboard light comes on. This helps you find the keys in the dark.

This is off at the start. To turn it on, turn on **Backlight on touch** in **Keyboard**.

## Switch to a different keyboard

Hold Sym, then choose a keyboard from the list.
