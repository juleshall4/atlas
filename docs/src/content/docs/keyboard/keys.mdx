## Tap keys

Touch these keys lightly to type them, without pressing. Choose which keys in **Keyboard**. Tap keys only type when Swipe typing is on.

## Word suggestions

As you type, the strip above the keyboard shows words and spelling corrections. Touch a word to type it.

## Full stop

Press the space bar twice.

## Shift and Alt

Press once for the next letter only. Press twice quickly to lock it, and once more to unlock.

Hold Alt and press a key to use an [action](/keyboard/shortcuts/#actions).

## Delete a word

Hold Alt and press Backspace. Ctrl and Backspace also work.

## More symbols

When Alt is on, the strip shows symbols. Touch a symbol to type it. Swipe left or right on the keys to see more.

## Emoji

Sym works like Shift. The strip shows emoji, with your recent emoji first. Swipe left or right on the keys to see more, or up or down for the next group.

## Find an emoji

Press Sym, then type a word, such as "pizza". The strip shows emoji that match. Press Enter to type the first one, or touch the one you want. Press Sym again to stop.

If no emoji match, the space bar types your words as text.

## Keyboard light

Turn on Backlight on touch in **Keyboard**. Then, outside a text field, slide your finger on the keys to turn on the keyboard light.

## Change the keyboard

Hold Sym, then select a different keyboard from the list.
