## Swipe a word

Slide your finger across the letters without pressing the keys, then lift it to type the word.

## Turn off swiping

Turn off Swipe typing in **Keyboard**. Swipes and tap keys stop typing words, but you can still move the cursor.

<Setting path={['Keyboard']} name="Swipe typing" />

## Continue after a lift

If your finger leaves the keys for a moment, the swipe carries on. You can change how long Atlas waits in **Keyboard**.

<Setting path={['Keyboard']} name="Continue after a lift" value="100 ms" />

## Pick another word

Touch a different word on the strip above the keyboard to type it instead.

## Move the cursor

Rest your finger on the space bar until you feel a click, then slide left or right.
