## Swipe a word

Put your finger on the first letter of the word and slide across the other letters, without pressing the keys. Lift your finger at the last letter, and Atlas types the word.

You do not need to be exact. Atlas looks at the shape of your swipe and picks the word that fits it best. Swiping works in any app where you can type.

Swipe typing is on after [setup](/#set-up-the-keyboard). Swipe recognition runs on your phone, and works in English only.

## Pick a different word

If Atlas types the wrong word, look at the strip above the keyboard. It shows the next best matches. Touch the word you meant, and Atlas swaps it in.

## Type one-letter words with a tap

You cannot swipe a word that has only one letter, such as "a" or "I". Tap keys let you type these letters with a light tap instead of a full press, so your hands can stay in swiping position.

To choose your tap keys, open **Keyboard** and select **Tap keys**, then press each key you want to add. Tap keys only work while swipe typing is on.

## Keep a swipe going if your finger slips

If your finger leaves the keys for a moment in the middle of a word, Atlas waits briefly before it ends the swipe. Put your finger back down and carry on.

To change how long Atlas waits, open **Keyboard** and select **Continue after a lift**. A longer time forgives bigger slips. A shorter time ends words faster. Choose **Off** to end every swipe as soon as you lift your finger.

## Feel each swipe

Atlas can give a light vibration as your finger moves across the keys. To change it, open **Keyboard** and select **Vibration**. You can choose **Normal**, **Strong** or **Off**, and try each one by swiping on the page.

This setting only changes swipe and tap key vibration. Atlas always gives a short vibration when you start moving the cursor or pick a word from the strip. To turn off all vibration, turn off **Touch feedback** in Android settings.

## Turn off swipe typing

If you only want to press keys, turn off **Swipe typing** in **Keyboard**. Swipes and tap keys stop typing, but you can still [move the cursor](/keyboard/keys/#move-the-cursor) with the space bar.
