---
title: Your words
description: Atlas learns the words you use, such as names and slang. You can add, block and back up your words.
---

## How Atlas learns words

When you type a word Atlas does not know 3 times, it saves the word. From then on, the word shows in suggestions and you can swipe it.

Atlas never learns from password, email, web address or private fields. Your words stay on your phone.

To stop Atlas learning new words, open **Keyboard**, select **Your words** and turn off **Learn my words**.

## See and add words

Open **Keyboard**, select **Your words**, then select **Saved words**. This shows every word Atlas has learned. To add a word yourself, type it at the top and press Enter. A word you add stays until you remove it.

## Block a word

If Atlas keeps suggesting a word you do not want, block it. In **Your words**, select **Blocked words**, type the word and press Enter. Atlas will not suggest or swipe a blocked word.

## Back up your words

To keep a copy of your words, for example before you move to a new phone, open **Your words** and select **Export words**. Atlas saves them to a file.

To add them back, select **Import words** and choose the file. Words you already have stay as they are.

## Start again

To remove every word Atlas has learned, select **Reset words** in **Your words**. You cannot undo this, so export your words first if you might want them back.
