---
title: Your words
description: Atlas learns the words you use. You can add, block and back up your words.
---

## New words

When you type a new word 3 times, Atlas learns it. A word you add yourself stays.

To see, add or block words, open **Keyboard** and select **Your words**.

## Back up your words

To keep a copy of your words, open **Your words** and select **Export words**. To add them back, for example on a new phone, select **Import words**.
