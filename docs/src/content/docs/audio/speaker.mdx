## Better media

The Titan 2 Elite's speaker is small, so music and video can sound thin, and loud sounds can crackle. Better media adjusts the sound before it reaches the speaker. Voices and music sound fuller, and the speaker stays clear at high volume.

Open **Audio** and turn on **Better media**.

<Setting path={['Audio']} name="Better media" on />

Better media only changes the phone speaker. Headphones and Bluetooth speakers sound the same as before.
