## Version 2.0.0

### New

- **Quick open:** hold Fn and press a letter to open an app.
- **Actions:** hold Alt and press a key to undo, redo, copy, cut, paste or select all.
- **Emoji search:** press Sym and type a word to find an emoji.
- **Skin tone:** pick a skin tone for emoji with hands and people in **Keyboard**.
- **Keyboard light:** slide your finger on the keys outside a text field to turn on the keyboard light.
- **Audio:** the phone speaker sounds fuller and clearer for media, and voices are clearer in app calls.

### Changed

- The home page shows Keyboard, Shortcuts and Audio. You can use Atlas before setup is finished.
- **Finish setup** shows in each feature that needs it, with only the steps that feature needs.
- To try the keyboard, open **Keyboard** and touch the keyboard icon at the top.
- Slip recovery period is now called Continue after a lift, and Swipe vibration is now called Vibration.
- Fn always opens apps, so the Hyper key setting is gone.
- When swipe typing is off, tap keys do not type.

### Fixed

- A notification at the top of the screen no longer catches swipes that start on the top row of keys.
- Atlas finds common words with double letters, such as "coffee", more often.
- Atlas gives more weight to the key where your swipe ends.
- Only a held Alt with Backspace deletes a word. A tapped or locked Alt with Backspace deletes one letter.
