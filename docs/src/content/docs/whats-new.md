## Version 2.0.0

Atlas 2.0 adds shortcuts for opening apps and editing text, emoji search and better speaker sound. The home page is new, and you can start using Atlas before setup is finished.

### New

- **Quick open:** give an app a letter, then hold Fn and press that letter to open it from anywhere. [Set up Quick open](/keyboard/shortcuts/#open-apps-with-quick-open).
- **Actions:** hold Alt and press Z, Y, A, C, X or V to undo, redo, select all, copy, cut or paste.
- **Emoji search:** press Sym and type a word, such as "pizza", to find an emoji.
- **Skin tone:** choose a skin tone for emoji with hands and people.
- **Keyboard light:** slide your finger on the keys to turn on the keyboard light.
- **Better media:** the speaker sounds fuller and clearer, without distorting at high volume.
- **Clearer calls:** voices are easier to hear in WhatsApp and other app calls on the speaker.

### Changed

- The home page shows Keyboard, Shortcuts and Audio, with Help below.
- Setup no longer blocks the app. **Finish setup** shows in each feature that still needs a step, and lists only the steps that feature needs.
- To try the keyboard, open **Keyboard** and touch the keyboard icon at the top.
- Every settings page uses the same text size.
- Slip recovery period is now called Continue after a lift. Swipe vibration is now called Vibration.
- Fn always opens apps, so the Hyper key setting is gone.
- When swipe typing is off, tap keys do not type, and Keyboard hides the swipe typing settings.

### Fixed

- Notifications at the top of the screen no longer catch swipes that start on the top row of keys.
- Common words with double letters, such as "coffee", are recognised more often.
- The key where your swipe ends counts for more, so the last letter is right more often.
- Alt and Backspace delete a whole word only when you hold Alt. A tapped or locked Alt deletes one letter.
