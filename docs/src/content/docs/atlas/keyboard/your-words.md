---
title: Your words
description: Atlas learns the words you use, such as names and slang. You can add, block and back up your words.
---

## How Atlas learns words

When you type a new word 3 times, Atlas saves it. Then the word shows in suggestions and you can swipe it. Atlas never learns from password, email, web address or private fields.

To stop Atlas learning new words, open **Keyboard**, select **Your words** and turn off **Learn my words**.

## See and add words

Open **Keyboard**, select **Your words**, then select **Saved words** to see every word Atlas has learned. To add a word, type it at the top and press Enter. A word you add stays until you remove it.

## Block a word

To stop Atlas suggesting a word, block it. In **Your words**, select **Blocked words**, type the word and press Enter. Atlas will not suggest or swipe a blocked word.

## Back up your words

To keep a copy of your words, for example before you move to a new phone, open **Your words** and select **Export words**. To add them back, select **Import words** and choose the file. Words you already have stay as they are.

## Start again

To remove every word Atlas has learned, select **Reset words** in **Your words**. You cannot undo this, so export your words first if you might want them back.
