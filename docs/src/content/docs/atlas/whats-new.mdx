<Release
	version="2.0.0"
	date="14 November 2026"
	summary="Shortcuts for opening apps and editing text, emoji search and better speaker sound. You can start using Atlas before setup is finished."
	latest
>
<Changes type="new">

- **Quick open** Hold Fn and press a letter to open an app from anywhere. [Set it up](/atlas/keyboard/shortcuts/#open-apps-with-quick-open)
- **Actions** Hold Alt and press Z, Y, A, C, X or V to undo, redo, select all, copy, cut or paste.
- **Emoji search** Press Sym and type a word, such as "pizza".
- **Skin tone** Choose a skin tone for emoji with hands and people.
- **Keyboard light** Slide your finger on the keys to turn it on.
- **Better media** The speaker sounds fuller, without distorting at high volume.
- **Clearer calls** Voices are easier to hear in app calls on the speaker.

</Changes>
<Changes type="changed">

- New home page with Keyboard, Shortcuts and Audio, and Help below.
- Setup no longer blocks the app. Each feature shows **Finish setup** until its steps are done.
- To try the keyboard, open **Keyboard** and touch the keyboard icon at the top.
- Slip recovery period is now **Continue after a lift**. Swipe vibration is now **Vibration**.
- Every settings page uses the same text size.
- Fn always opens apps, so the Hyper key setting is gone.
- When swipe typing is off, tap keys do not type.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- Notifications no longer catch swipes that start on the top row of keys.
- Words with double letters, such as "coffee", are right more often.
- The last letter of a swipe is right more often.
- Alt and Backspace delete a whole word only when you hold Alt.

</Changes>
</Release>

<EarlierVersions>
<Release version="1.3.1" date="9 November 2026" summary="Long swipes work again.">
<Changes type="fixed">

- Long swipes, such as "investigating", work again. Version 1.3.0 sometimes mistook one finger for two and stopped the swipe.

</Changes>
</Release>
<Release version="1.3.0" date="9 November 2026" summary="Switch keyboards with Sym.">
<Changes type="new">

- **Switch keyboards** Hold Sym to choose a different keyboard. A short press on Sym still shows emoji.
- **Lowercase start** At the start of a sentence, press Shift to type a lowercase letter, for words such as "iPhone".

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- Atlas no longer starts a new sentence after "e.g.", "i.e." or a full stop with no space after it, such as in "atlas.com".
- After you type with the keys, Atlas waits 0.4 seconds before a tap key types, the cursor moves or swipe vibration starts.
- Two fingers on the keys no longer type a swipe or move the cursor.
- Saved words show in the strip in their normal order, not always first.
- Touching a word on the strip gives the same vibration as starting the cursor.
- When swipe typing is off, the settings for swiping are hidden.

</Changes>
</Release>
<Release version="1.2.0" date="9 November 2026" summary="Emoji and spelling corrections.">
<Changes type="new">

- **Emoji** Press Sym to show emoji in the strip, with your recent emoji first.
- **Spelling corrections** The strip shows the correct spelling of a misspelled word.
- **Better suggestions** The strip suggests more words, with the best match in the middle.
- **Longer words first** When you type with the keys, "deli" suggests "delicious" and "delight".
- **Next words** After you touch a word on the strip, the next suggestions show straight away.
- **Delete a word** Hold Alt and press Backspace.
- **More symbols** When Alt is on, a short swipe on the keys shows more symbols.
- **Punctuation** Atlas removes a space before punctuation, and fixes "i" to "I" and "dont" to "don't".
- **Auto-punctuation** Turn this setting off to stop these changes.
- **Swipe typing** Turn this setting off to stop swipes typing words. Cursor control still works.
- **More fields** The strip shows in address bars and private fields. Atlas does not learn words from them.
- **Starter words** When you select all the text in a search bar, the strip suggests words to start with.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- Words with a typo are not saved. Atlas saves a new word after you use it 3 times.
- Atlas forgets a word you delete straight after typing it.
- A finger resting on the keys while you type no longer starts a swipe or types a tap key.
- The strip no longer flickers while new suggestions load.
- The strip is hidden in password, email and number fields.
- To move the cursor, lift your finger after pressing the space bar, then rest it again.
- Atlas no longer vibrates when the cursor is already at the start or end of the text.
- Screen readers read the best suggestion first.
- Pressing Ctrl or Meta on its own no longer sends a shortcut to the app.
- The strip no longer stays on the screen after an app closes a text field.
- When you go back to an app, its text field stays open.

</Changes>
</Release>
<Release version="1.1.0" date="8 November 2026" summary="Move the cursor from the space bar.">
<Changes type="new">

- **Move the cursor** Rest your finger on the space bar until you feel a click, then slide left or right.
- **Update notes** After an update, Atlas shows what changed.
- **Setup** New steps help you set up Scroll assistant and turn on Atlas in Accessibility.
- **Guide** Simpler instructions, and help with setup problems.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- One back swipe closes the strip and goes back.
- Atlas starts a text field with a capital letter, even when the field has hidden characters at the start.

</Changes>
</Release>
<Release version="1.0.1" date="8 November 2026" summary="The strip stays hidden on the home screen.">
<Changes type="fixed">

- The strip stays hidden on your launcher's home screen, so typing to search stays clean.
- The strip no longer comes back after an app closes a text box.

</Changes>
</Release>
<Release version="1.0.0" date="8 November 2026" summary="Swipe typing on the Titan 2 Elite's keys.">
<Changes type="new">

- **First version** Swipe typing on the Titan 2 Elite's physical keys.

</Changes>
</Release>
