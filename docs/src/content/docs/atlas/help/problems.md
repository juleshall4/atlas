---
title: Problems
description: What to do when the keyboard or a shortcut does not work as you expect.
---

## The keyboard does not work properly

Most problems come from a setup step that is not done. Open Atlas and select **Keyboard**. If you see **Finish setup**, select it and do the steps it shows.

## Swiping does not work in one app

In some apps, swipes on the keys scroll the page instead of typing. In the phone's Settings, open **Scroll assistant**, find the app and set it to **Sliding Mode 1**.

## Sym does not show emoji

The Sym key may be set to do something else. In the phone's Settings, open **Shortcut keys** and set **Sym key** to **Programmable key**.

## Quick open does not open an app

Check that **Quick open** is on in **Shortcuts**, and that the letter is given to that app in **Apps**. If **Shortcuts** shows **Finish setup**, select it and turn on Atlas in Accessibility.

## A notification catches my swipes

Open **Keyboard**, select **Finish setup** and turn on Atlas in Accessibility. Swipes on the top row then go to the keyboard, not the notification.

## Something else is wrong

[Send feedback](/atlas/help/feedback/) and say what you did and what happened.
