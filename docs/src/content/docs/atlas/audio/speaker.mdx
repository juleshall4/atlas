## Better media

The Titan 2 Elite's small speaker can sound thin and crackle when loud. Better media makes music, video and voices sound fuller, and keeps the speaker clear at high volume.

Open **Audio** and turn on **Better media**.

<Setting path={['Audio']} name="Better media" on />

Headphones and Bluetooth speakers do not change.
