# 1.4.0

## App
- Quick open: Hold Fn and push a letter to open an app.
- Actions: Hold Alt and push a key to undo, redo, copy, cut, paste or select all.
- Home: The home screen shows Keyboard, Shortcuts and Audio. You can use Atlas before setup is complete.
- Emoji search: Push Sym and type a word to find an emoji.
- Audio: Atlas can tune the speaker for media and app calls.

## New
- Quick open: Hold Fn and push a letter to open an app. Or push Fn, then push the letter in 1 second. To set a letter, open Shortcuts, then Apps. Select an app and push a letter. To remove the letter, select the app and push Backspace. To open apps with no animation, set Animation to off. Quick open is off at the start. To use it, open Shortcuts and set Quick open to on.
- Actions: Push and hold Alt, then push a key. Z is undo. Y is redo. A selects all text. C is copy. X is cut. V is paste. Backspace deletes a word. Only Delete word is on at the start. To turn an action on or off, open Shortcuts, then Keys. When an action is off, a held Alt and its key type a symbol.
- Emoji search: Push Sym, then type a word, for example "pizza". The strip shows the emoji that match. Push Enter to type the first emoji, or touch an emoji. When Sym is locked, the strip stays open for the next search. If no emoji match, the space bar and Enter type your words as text.
- Skin tone: To change the skin tone of emoji with hands and people, open Keyboard, then Skin tone.
- Keyboard light: When you slide your finger on the keys outside a text field, the keyboard light comes on. This is off at the start. To use it, open Keyboard and set Backlight on touch to on.
- Audio: Atlas can tune the speaker for media, such as music, video and games. Open Audio and set Media to on. Headphones and Bluetooth do not change. To tune WhatsApp and other app calls, open Audio and do the step below Calls.

## Changed
- Sym: When Sym is on, letters find emoji. Backspace with no search closes the emoji.
- Home: The home screen shows Keyboard, Shortcuts and Audio, and Help below them. Setup does not block the app. When keyboard setup is not complete, Finish setup shows at the top of Keyboard.
- Preview: To try the keyboard, open Keyboard and touch the keyboard icon at the top.
- Text size: All settings use the same text size.
- Names: Slip recovery period is now Continue after a lift. Swipe vibration is now Vibration.
- Fn: Fn is always the key to open apps. The Hyper key setting is removed.
- Tap keys: When swipe typing is off, tap keys do not type.
- Vibration: This setting changes only swipe and tap key vibration. Atlas always vibrates when you start the cursor, move it or select a suggestion. To stop all vibration, set Touch feedback to off in Android settings.
- Continue after a lift: This setting changes only swipe typing. Cursor control always continues after a lift of 90 ms or less.

## Fixed
- Notifications: When a notification shows at the top of the screen, your swipes go to the keyboard. Before, the notification got swipes that started on the top row of keys. Atlas must be on in Accessibility for this fix.
- Saved words: When you save a word with a double letter, such as "supp", the strip continues to show the word that you swiped before.
- Double letters: When you swipe a common word with a double letter, such as "coffee", Atlas finds the word more often.
- Swipe end: Atlas gives more importance to the key where your swipe stops.
- Delete a word: Only a held Alt with Backspace deletes a word. A tapped or locked Alt with Backspace deletes one letter.
- Keyboard: When swipe typing is off, Keyboard does not show Tap keys, Vibration and Continue after a lift. When Quick open is off, Shortcuts does not show its settings.
